1. Information We Collect

We temporarily collect the video files you upload solely for the purpose of removing background noise using our AI models. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your uploaded files are processed strictly to provide the voice isolation service. The processing is done securely, and your files are temporarily stored on our servers only for the duration of the background noise removal process.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

We respect your privacy and data security. All uploaded original files and processed output files are automatically and permanently deleted from our servers shortly after you download the result, or within 24 hours of upload, whichever comes first.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Meta Demucs AI for audio processing. However, all processing is performed on our own infrastructure. We do not send your audio or video data to Meta, Facebook, or any other external third-party APIs for processing.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
